  <h2>COFOG-Panel: an open-source tool for government expenditure data</h2>
  <p class="card__label">Description</p>
  <p>COFOG-Panel is a traceable Python tool that builds cross-country panels of government expenditure by function (COFOG) from IMF Government Finance Statistics. Every cleaning and harmonization step is documented so that the resulting panels can be reproduced and audited. Version 2.0.0 is in development, and contributors and users are welcome.</p>
  <p class="card__label">Methods and data</p>
  <p>Open-source Python development on GitHub; automated retrieval, cleaning and harmonization of IMF GFS expenditure-by-function data across countries and years, with a log that traces each figure back to its source.</p>

  <h2>Circular bamboo agroforestry on former rubber land</h2>
  <p class="card__label">Description</p>
  <p>This project studies how former rubber plantations in Southeast Vietnam can be converted into circular agroforestry systems built around bamboo, combining trees, crops, forage and livestock on the same land. It assesses the economic returns, labor requirements and environmental benefits of these systems compared with monoculture, and what they imply for rural livelihoods and land restoration.</p>
  <p class="card__label">Methods and data</p>
  <p>Farm-level cost-benefit and cash-flow analysis, enterprise budgets for alternative combinations of bamboo, crops and livestock, and material-flow accounting of nutrient and biomass loops; primary data from a pilot site in Southeast Vietnam, complemented by secondary data on bamboo and agricultural value chains.</p>
</article>
<article class="card reveal">
  <h2>Provincial input-output tables and high-tech agriculture</h2>
  <p class="card__label">Description</p>
  <p>This project builds input-output tables at the provincial level in Vietnam, starting with Lâm Đồng province, and uses them to simulate how the development of high-tech agriculture affects growth, employment and inter-sectoral linkages. It extends our 171-sector regional input-output table for Ho Chi Minh City.</p>
  <p class="card__label">Methods and data</p>
  <p>Regionalization of the national input-output table using the Flegg location quotient (FLQ), cross-hauling adjustment following Kronenberg and RAS balancing; disaggregation of household income by quintile; multiplier, linkage and impact simulation analysis. Data come from the national input-output table of the General Statistics Office, provincial statistical yearbooks, and enterprise and household surveys.</p>
</article>
